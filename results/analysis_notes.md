# Initial Analysis

Latest shared reporting year: 2024.

Counties with usable housing values and incomes: 3,086.

Median home-value-to-income ratio: 2.923.

Correlation of unemployment and home-value-to-income ratio (counties with both): 0.050.

The home-value-to-income ratio compares median owner-occupied home value to median household income. A higher ratio suggests less affordability by this simple measure, but it does not represent a mortgage payment or local rent. These associations should not be interpreted as causation.
